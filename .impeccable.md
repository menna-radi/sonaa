## Design Context

### Users
Internal Sonaa operations teams use the dashboard to monitor tasks, craftsmen, payments, subscriptions, disputes, safety reports, notifications, and live activity. During busy periods, the highest-priority job is making correct operational decisions quickly without losing trust in system status or audit context.

### Brand Personality
Calm, trustworthy, and operationally clear. The interface should reduce cognitive load rather than create visual urgency except for genuine safety, payment, or service incidents.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined operational tooling with strong hierarchy, restrained surfaces, readable status signals, and bilingual English/Arabic support. Preserve dark and light theme support, but prioritize legibility, contrast, and calm over decorative effects.

### Design Principles
- Make operational state and next actions immediately scannable.
- Use color and motion to communicate meaning, not decoration.
- Keep payment, moderation, and safety workflows auditable and reversible.
- Design every workflow for keyboard access, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, and responsive use.
- Prefer consistent tokens and predictable layouts over one-off page styling.
